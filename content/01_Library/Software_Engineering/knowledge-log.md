> Knowledge log adalah weekly reflection tool.

## Template
```markdown
# Knowledge Log - Week XX

## Achievements
- [ ] Topic 1: Learned X -> selesai/exercise/belum
- [ ] Topic 2: Built Y -> selesai/exercise/belum

## Reflection
- What clicked?
- What was confusing?
- Connection to vault: [[related-note]]

## Next Week
- Topic A (continued)
- Topic B (new)
```

## Tips
- 3 bullet points per week minimum
- Link every achievement to vault notes
- Don't overthink - just write
