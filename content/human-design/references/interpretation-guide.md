## Authority (Decision-Making)

Process in this order — highest authority overrides lower:

1. **Emotional / Solar Plexus** — Wait through the emotional wave; clarity comes with time. Never decide in the high or low.
2. **Sacral** — Gut response in the moment. Yes/no sounds (uh-huh / un-uh). Only for Generators/MGs.
3. **Splenic** — In-the-moment intuition. Quiet, one-time signal. Easy to override — that's the mistake.
4. **Ego/Heart** — Decisions based on what they want and can commit to. "Do I really want this?"
5. **Self-Projected (G Center)** — Talking to trusted others to hear themselves. Not asking for advice.
6. **Mental / Environmental** — No inner authority. Decision-making through environment and trusted sounding boards.
7. **Lunar (Reflectors only)** — Track the 28-day lunar cycle. Sample input from many environments before deciding.

## Gates & I-Ching Correspondence

The 64 gates map directly to 64 I-Ching hexagrams. The gate number IS the hexagram number in Ra's system.

**Example — Gate 1 (Hexagram 1: The Creative)**
- Keyword: Self-Expression
- Channel: 1-8 (Inspiration → Contribution)
- Circuit: Individual

**When writing gate interpretations:**
- Use the gate keyword + its shadow/gift/siddhi framing only if working within the Gene Keys system (different from HD core)
- Core HD gate keywords: use Ra Uru Hu's original keywords, NOT alternative systems unless specified
- Always note when gate meaning is interpretive vs. structural

## Channels (36 Total)

Channels are defined when BOTH gates at either end are activated. A single gate activation = "hanging gate" — potential, not definition.

**Circuits:**
- **Individual** — Mutation, empowerment, melancholy; not meant for the collective
- **Collective** — Sharing, logic (pattern recognition) or sensing (past experience)
- **Tribal** — Support, bargaining, community survival

## Incarnation Cross

Formed by 4 gates:
- Conscious Sun (personality — what you're aware of)
- Conscious Earth (grounding force)
- Unconscious Sun (design — body's purpose)
- Unconscious Earth (unconscious grounding)

768 possible crosses grouped into 4 quarters:
- **Quarter of Initiation** — Purpose through Mind
- **Quarter of Civilization** — Purpose through Form
- **Quarter of Duality** — Purpose through Bonding
- **Quarter of Mutation** — Purpose through Transformation

> [Keyakinan sedang] Incarnation Cross names and interpretations vary significantly between practitioners. Flag to user when interpretation is lineage-specific.
